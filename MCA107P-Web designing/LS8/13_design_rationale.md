# Design rationale

> Prepare a comprehensive design rationale document explaining color choices, typography, and target audience alignment.

## Audience and tone

The imagined bakery serves local residents, casual visitors, and people seeking a welcoming, neighborhood-scale food experience. Its voice is warm, calm, and direct: concise descriptions, seasonal details, and clear practical information make the page useful before it becomes decorative.

## Color

Forest green connects the identity to ingredients, plants, and craft while creating a strong surface for navigation and reviews. Warm cream gives the menu and hero a paper-like base. Sage marks the story and illustrations without relying on photographic stock art. Terracotta brings warmth to the reservation action and small editorial accents. Butter-gold is reserved for decorative details and large accents rather than small body copy.

Contrast remains intentional: body copy uses deep ink on cream/white, controls use dark text on a light field, and the dark review band uses white text and pale gold headings. The accessibility checklist recommends verifying actual contrast pairs in an analyzer rather than trusting color names.

## Type and composition

A Georgia display face adds editorial character to headlines and testimonials; familiar system sans-serif body text improves scanability and avoids a remote font request. Fluid headings, restrained line lengths, and repeatable spacing tokens create a readable hierarchy. The mobile-first composition prioritizes the menu, appointment call-to-action, and contact details, then uses additional screen width for paired story/map layouts and three-column cards.

## Image and motion

Small custom SVG drawings avoid generic photography, reduce transfer size, and remain crisp at all resolutions. Descriptive alternative text communicates meaningful artwork; decorative marks and repeated portraits are hidden from assistive technology where adjacent text already conveys meaning. Carousel movement is manually controllable, pauseable, and disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

## Trust and prototype boundaries

The brand, staff, menu, prices, address, telephone number, and customer comments are fictional and labeled as examples. The reservation demo gives an on-page confirmation but does not book, email, transmit, or store anything. Sharing uses the browser's native share mechanism; external social links do not claim that a real brand account or feed exists.
